## Build docs
- https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/main/docs/native-mods.md (5.5k lines), section "Pinned to a Nightly" + "Mods on the hook points".
- Mod builds on exact commit in `[compatibility] horizon_revision` of horizon-mixin.toml; each nightly keeps source forever as tag nightly-source/<date>-<short>. Mixed pins: launcher prompts "Build anyway" (`native-build --allow-version-mismatch`); `--ignore-pins` ("Rebuild on Horizon") builds all on picked version commit. Mixins pin: newest of mods' and launcher's.
- Mods with `"hooks": N` in horizon-native.json are not pinned: build on any commit with NATIVE_HOOKS_API>=N; hooks only ever added (additive), launcher sets horizon_revision in its copy. horizon-mixins refuses mods with E_TARGET_NOT_FOUND/E_MODULE/E_HOOK_CATALOG; other mods still build; server engine fails instead.
- Mods that touch engine internals without hooks (pin only) break often: 21 of 133 broke on fdc87ae vs 11-0x; hook-based ones are stable.
- CLI: launcher `native-build`; cargo-horizon-mod; official mod source native/tools/native.py (check/package/proof).
