## 3. Bench method and noise

- Harness: `tools/horizon-offline-test.py` plus matrix runners under `outputs/horizon-runtime/`. Cow scene, RD 12 / sim 8, fps cap 260 (uncapped in practice), VSync off, warmup 15 s, static 20 s, flight 16 s. [ab-r2, bench-method]
- Gates before every run: AC power, screen unlocked, no cargo/rustc/xcodebuild/PineconeMC/Java MC, at least 4 GiB free disk, thermal check, 60 s cooldown between runs. All runs through the device queue. [ab-r2, r3-bisect]
- Design: ABBA/BAAB interleaving, consecutive runs form pairs, median of paired deltas with 10,000-resample bootstrap 95% CI. With 4 pairs a CI is close to min..max, so treat it as fragile. [ab-r2, r2-makeup-p99-analysis]
- Resolution differs by doc: ab-r2, overrides, r3 bisect used a 1280x720 window; ab-cloud-shadow and ab-v4 report a 2560x1440 surface. Compare deltas inside one doc, not absolute FPS across docs. [ab-r2, ab-v4, ab-cloud-shadow]
- Noise: shader-off avg FPS SD about 19 (range 40 at n=4); MakeUp SD about 2.7 (n=2, probably low). 1% lows swing 5-12 FPS in single pairs. Swap was 2.5-6 GB used in every bench; thermal pressure level 1-2 in ab-v4. [bench-method, overrides-matrix, ab-v4]
- Repeats needed (power 0.8): shader off, 10 FPS needs 57 runs per arm; MakeUp 3 FPS needs 13 per arm. So prefer GPU ms and MakeUp for A/B. [bench-method]
- Limits: the harness does not save the static per-frame series, so p99 tails cannot be split into spikes vs drift. [r3-bisect]

## 5b. Shader packs: what Horizon supports and what to test next (research 2026-11-06)

- f42 loads any Iris/OptiFine GLSL pack through a generic GLSL-to-Naga translator (`crates/horizon-shaders/src`, about 15.6k lines). No per-pack ports exist. The translator carries pack-specific fixes for MakeUp (`translate/mod.rs:1699`), Miniature (`:2147`), BSL/Complementary/Solas (`:2215`) and Mellow (`:2705`), so those packs were the development targets. Selection: `pickedShaderPack:<zip>` in `horizon-graphics.txt`. Option values go in `shaderpacks/<pack>.txt`. Per-pass GPU timing: F3 or `HORIZON_SHADER_PACK_TIMING`.
- Shadow cost knobs come from each pack's own `const` lines (`shadowMapResolution`, `shadowDistance`, `shadowDistanceRenderMul`, `config.rs:124-128`). Quality profiles come from the pack's `shaders.properties`.
- The harness already knows 10 packs (`tools/horizon-offline-test.py:18-22`). U9 tested only 4 because those were the earlier bench cases. **U9b** (`run-u9b-shaders.py`, tag `u9b`, 12 runs, queued after U9) screens the other 6 with one paired round each, control+v4 vs r3+v4: I Like Vanilla, Super Duper Vanilla, Sildur's Vibrant Lite, BSL, Bliss, Complementary Unbound.
- **Upstream status (checked):** upstream `ROADMAP.md:263` still says shader packs "were removed and are being rebuilt from scratch", but that text is stale (it cites issues #1584-#1801). Main still has the translator: `crates/horizon-shaders/src/translate/mod.rs` was last changed 2026-11-05 (`4c44b577`, shadowtex sampler). Iris parity work continues in issues #10450-#10455, and #10418 reports shader-pack buffers doubling on window resize. A rebase would keep MakeUp. Native rewrites are planned for Photon, Chocapic13 and MakeUp Ultra Fast (each after a licence check), plus a light default pack (<0.3 ms). Open translation issues: #8174 (`flat` qualifier), #4786 (shadows), #9307.
- Candidates not installed yet (M1 data is anecdotal Reddit/Modrinth reports; no controlled benchmarks were found): Sildur's Enhanced Default and Baked Potato (cheap, vanilla-plus, closest to MakeUp's look), Chocapic13 lite (on Horizon's native roadmap), Photon (prettier, unmeasured cost), Solas (BSL-like; the translator already has a Solas fix). Mellow: one report of ~70 FPS on an M1 Air under Java. BSL and Complementary are reported as too heavy for an 8 GB Air.
- Downloaded 2026-11-06 with the user's OK (Modrinth, sha512 verified, shader source only): Baked Potato 2.0 (`BakedPotatoFinal.zip`, 26.3) and Sildur's Enhanced Default 1.20 Fancy (`Sildur's Enhanced Default v1.20 Fancy.zip`, 26.3). They are in `outputs/horizon-runtime/game/shaderpacks/` (Horizon only, not the Java instance). Harness keys: `bakedpotato` and `sildursdefault`. The harness falls back to that folder when a pack is not in the Java instance. U9b is now 16 runs. The earlier Modrinth TLS failure was transient.
